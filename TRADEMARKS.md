# Trademark policy

"Decirance" and the Decirance logo are trademarks of the project's owning
company. **An open-source licence covers copyright and patents. It does not
grant trademark rights**, and neither Apache 2.0 nor CC BY 4.0 grants any here.

This policy exists for one specific reason. Decirance's subject is
*authorisation* — deciding what an agent may do and recording who accepted the
risk. A vendor using the open code to imply that we assessed or approved their
product would be making exactly the kind of unearned assurance claim this
project was built to prevent. That is a worse outcome for us than someone
forking the code, which we welcome.

## You may

- State truthfully that your product, service or process **uses**, **is built
  on**, or **is compatible with** the Decirance open assurance framework.
- Use the word Decirance in plain text to refer to this project — in
  documentation, articles, talks, research and comparisons.
- Distribute unmodified copies of this repository under its licences.
- Say that your output conforms to a Decirance schema version, where it does.

Acceptable:

> Built using the Decirance open assurance framework.
> Agent Passport conforming to Decirance schema 0.1.0.
> We evaluated Decirance alongside two other approaches.

## You may not

- Use the name or logo in a way that implies endorsement, partnership,
  certification or approval that has not been granted.
- Name a modified version, fork or derivative product "Decirance", or use a
  confusingly similar name.
- Present a fork as the official Decirance project or platform.
- Use the logo as a certification mark, badge or seal on a product, agent or
  assessment.
- Claim any status such as "Decirance Certified", "Decirance Approved" or
  "Decirance Verified" without written authorisation. No such programme
  currently exists.
- Register the name or a confusingly similar mark, or use it in a domain name
  or app name suggesting official status.

Not acceptable:

> Decirance-certified agent.
> Decirance Approved.
> Decirance Enterprise Edition *(for a third-party fork)*.

## Forks

Fork freely — that is what the licence is for. Give it your own name, and say
what it derives from:

> Acme Assurance, a fork of the Decirance open assurance framework.

Please also remove the Decirance logo from a fork's own branding. Keeping the
name in attribution and history is expected and welcome; using it as the fork's
identity is not.

## A permit is not a certification

The Deployment Permit produced by this software records **an organisation's own
accountable owner authorising their own agent**. It is not a Decirance
certification, and Decirance is not a certification body, regulator, notified
body or accreditation authority.

Presenting a permit as though Decirance issued or validated it misrepresents
both the artefact and this project.

## Questions

Uses not covered here, or a request for written authorisation, go to
**trademarks@decirance.com**. We would rather answer a question than send a
complaint.
